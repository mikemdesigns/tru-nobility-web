## Sun Ra transparent PNG

Use case: background-extraction. Input image: edit target, the supplied archival black-and-white Sun Ra photograph. Create a transparent PNG cutout of Sun Ra only, removing the wall and all background around his head and shoulders. Preserve his exact identity, upward gaze, large glasses, hat, face, expression, original photographic grain, deep blacks, ornate metallic costume and framing. Keep the full width of his visible garment through the bottom edge. Change only the background; do not redraw or beautify him, smooth skin, invent features, or add elements. Genuine alpha transparency, no checkerboard painted into image, no white or black backdrop, no shadow, no text.

## Saturn background

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: panoramic website background for an editorial Freedom Studies feature about Sun Ra. Generate a black-and-white photographic space scene, wide 3:1 landscape. Deep near-black space, fine scattered white stars with subtle archival film grain, a beautiful recognizable Saturn with tilted rings in the far right quarter, planet vertically near center so it remains visible in a shallow banner crop. Left and center remain mostly dark with sparse stars to allow white headings and a foreground portrait. Saturn is softly lit silver gray with a shadowed hemisphere, elegant atmospheric depth. Restrained contrast, rich black, exclusively grayscale. No text, people, logos, additional planets or colorful nebulae. Background artwork only, not a mockup.
